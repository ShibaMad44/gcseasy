How XP works
  • Exam questions: 1 XP per mark you get, once per question.
  • Daily challenge: 2 XP per correct answer, +5 for finishing, +5 more for 5/5.
  • Flashcards: 1 XP for each card you get right when it's due (up to 150 a day).
  • Quizzes, Maths practice, vocab and quote tests, calc drills: 1 XP per right answer (up to 60–80 a day each).
  • Revision timer: 1 XP for every 5 minutes.
  • Level up as your total grows. The leaderboard ranks by XP earned in the last 7 days.

How to revise with GCSEasy

Work through the tabs on a subject page in order. Most people need two or three sessions per topic.

  1. Read the notesGo through the topic once. "Triple" and "HT" mark content that isn't on every paper.
  2. Test with flashcardsKeep going until the deck is empty. Cards you get wrong come back.
  3. Learn the techniqueSee how examiners want answers written. That's worth more marks than any single fact.
  4. Do exam questionsWrite your answer, then mark it. Your weakest topic shows up in the score bars.
