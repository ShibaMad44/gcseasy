Best free GCSE resources

The videos, sites and past papers that actually help, sorted by subject. They're free to use; a few need a school login or only have a limited free version, and those say so. YouTube links may be blocked on some school networks, so watch those at home.

How to use them well

  • Watch, then test. After a video, close it and write down or say what you remember. Then check. Watching alone feels productive but doesn't stick.
  • Past papers are king. Once a topic is learned, do exam questions on it and mark them with the mark scheme. Read the examiner report to see where people lose marks.
  • Little and often. 25 minutes of focus and a 5-minute break beats a 3-hour cram. Use the timer in the header.
